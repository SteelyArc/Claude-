# Six new styles (concepts 5–10)

Every sample is a real render from the production engine: 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264, 10.5 s, silent.
To make the styles directly comparable, all six tell **the same beat**:

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 s | Hook title: *What if Poland–Lithuania had never been partitioned?* |
| 2.0–3.2 s | The Commonwealth before the partitions (c. 1770) |
| 3.2–4.4 s | 1772 First Partition (Prussia, Russia, Austria) |
| 4.4–5.6 s | 1793 Second Partition (Prussia, Russia) |
| 5.6–6.8 s | 1795 Third Partition (Prussia, Russia, Austria): the state disappears |
| 6.8–8.0 s | Pivot: *But what if the reforms of 1791 had survived?* |
| 8.0–10.5 s | Alternate timeline: the Commonwealth survives in its **1791 borders** |

The same rules apply to all six:
- Borders come from the same verified historical layers as the final film.
- In the alternate timeline, the 1772 losses stay with the powers that took them, because the scenario starts from the 1791 state.
- A status label switches from **DOCUMENTED HISTORY** to **HYPOTHETICAL** at the pivot.
- The 1791 border is always dashed or marked as hypothetical.
- There is no branding.

## 5 – Copperplate
`concept5_copperplate.mp4`

An 18th-century hand-engraved, hand-coloured map comes alive under a slow rostrum camera.
- **Paper:** laid paper with chain lines and foxing.
- **Engraving:** engraved water-lines ring every coast, and ruled hatching fills the sea.
- **Borders:** dotted engraved border lines, with the period practice of *outline colouring*: a watercolour band painted just inside each border.
- **Type:** engraved title and year cartouches.
- **Partitions:** each annexation blooms in as wet watercolour from the annexing power's side. In 1795 the Commonwealth's name is struck through in ink.
- **Alternate timeline:** a quill re-draws the 1791 border.
- **Mood:** scholarly and beautiful, like an antique atlas.

## 6 – Stained Glass
`concept6_stained_glass.mp4`

The map is a backlit lancet window in a dark stone nave.
- **Window:** every state is cut into shards of tinted glass, and the real borders are heavy lead cames.
- **Light:** shafts of light and drifting dust fall into the nave, with bloom and halation.
- **Partitions:** each one re-glazes the window shard by shard from the annexing side, with a flash on every shard.
- **Pivot and alternate timeline:** when the state is gone the light dies. At the pivot the window relights, and the 1791 Commonwealth glows in crimson and gold.
- **Mood:** solemn, emotional, iconic. Very strong in the first seconds.

## 7 – Paper Toy Theatre
`concept7_toy_theatre.mp4`

A handmade stop-motion paper theatre animated "on twos" (12 drawings per second) with a gentle paper boil.
- **Map:** layered hand-torn card with real cast shadows.
- **Stage:** a cut-paper proscenium, a scalloped pelmet and wave-strip seas.
- **Partitions:** annexed land is literally torn out of the Commonwealth and carried off towards the annexing power.
- **Pivot and alternate timeline:** paper curtains close on 1795 and reopen on the alternate timeline, where the 1791 state is stitched in gold thread.
- **Labels:** paper tapes.
- **Mood:** warm, charming, very "made by hand", and stands out in a feed.

## 8 – Pop Poster
`concept8_pop_poster.mp4`

Loud, flat and graphic, built for the scroll.
- **Print look:** a screen-printed poster on cream stock, with flat inks, heavy black key-lines, hard offset shadows and slight print mis-registration.
- **Motion:** kinetic type slams in with overshoot, the camera "punches" on every date, and a rotating sunburst drives the hook.
- **Partitions:** each annexed piece pops off with a bounce and flips to the annexing power's ink, while the power's name slams in beside it.
- **Mood:** maximum energy and readability; the most TikTok-native.

## 9 – Orbit
`concept9_orbit.mp4`

A space-documentary look on a real orthographic globe.
- **Hook:** dives from a full Earth onto Central Europe.
- **Globe:** atmosphere rim, sunlit and night shading, a star field, and a restrained HUD with live coordinates, altitude, the year and a Warsaw leader line.
- **Partitions:** spread across the curved surface as luminous fills.
- **Ending:** the alternate timeline lights the 1791 state, and the camera pulls back into orbit to show it on the planet.
- **Mood:** epic and modern; "the whole world would be different".

## 10 – The Case File
`concept10_case_file.mp4`

A true-crime evidence board.
- **Board:** cork and push-pins, genuine portraits and the 1773 satirical print as pinned photos, a plain map print-out, marker ink and red string.
- **Partitions:** each one is "solved" on the board. Red string runs from **the ruler who actually signed it** to the land taken, and the land is hatched in marker. The rulers are:
  - Frederick II for 1772, then Frederick William II for 1793 and 1795
  - Maria Theresa with Joseph II for 1772, then Francis II for 1795
  - Catherine II throughout
- **Alternate timeline:** the strings are cut, the Constitution painting is pinned up, a yellow **HYPOTHETICAL** note goes up, and the 1791 border is traced in highlighter ("CASE REOPENED").
- **Mood:** gripping and investigative; it frames the story as a mystery.
