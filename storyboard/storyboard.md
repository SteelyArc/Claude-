# Storyboard — West Prussia: A Region Shaped by History

All times are from the word-level transcript (`research/narration_transcript.json`). Scene lengths follow the narration rather than fixed slots.

Type: serif display for dates (e.g. Cormorant Garamond SemiBold), clean humanist sans for labels (e.g. Source Sans 3). Minimum label size 34 px; dates 96–140 px. All fonts are open-licence (OFL) and are bundled in the repo.

Map palette (atlas style): parchment `#EFE6D2`, sea `#C9D6D3` with fine engraving hatch, coast `#5B5A52`, rivers `#7FA1A8`. Territories in muted pastels: Teutonic Order *stone grey-ochre* `#D9C9A3`, Polish Crown / Poland *dusty rose* `#E3B9AE`, Prussia / Germany *slate blue-grey* `#B8C3CF`, Free City of Danzig *sage* `#BFD1B0`, Russia *pale lilac* `#CFC4D9`, Austria *wheat* `#E6D59C`, plebiscite area *hatched amber outline*. Highlights are drawn with an ink line, not saturated fills.
