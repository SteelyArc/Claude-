## Known data limits / decisions

- **Five villages (12 Aug 1920)** on the right bank of the Vistula assigned to Poland after the plebiscite are not modelled in OpenHistoricalMap (`five_villages_1920` = 0). They are **not drawn**; the fact is given in text only.
- **Draheim exclave** (≈16.3°E, annexed 1772): its provincial assignment in 1773 could not be verified, so it is **excluded** from the derived 1773 West Prussia polygon and shown only as Prussian territory.
- **West Prussia 1773** has no OHM relation; it is derived = Prussian 1772 gains − Warmia − Netze District + Kreis Marienwerder (en.wikipedia 'West Prussia': composition of the 1773 province).
- **Austrian/Russian 1772 shares** are derived from the Commonwealth before/after 1773 minus the Prussian share, split by location (south = Austria/Galicia, north-east = Russia).
- **Poland Jan/Aug 1920** relations are open in the east (border not yet fixed); northern maps use Poland 1924, whose northern border (Corridor/Free City) is identical to 1920.
- **1939 annexed area** = Reichsgau Danzig-West Prussia ∩ (Poland 1938 ∪ Free City); the Reichsgau's eastern part (Regierungsbezirk West Prussia) was already German and is not hatched.
- **Soviet zone 1945** = East Prussia (1922–39) − Poland 1945, so the two borders coincide exactly.
- **Vistula delta** is period-dependent (OHM dated waterways): pre-1895 course to Danzig; the 1895 cut only from 1895.
- **Thorn 1793–1807**: OHM places Thorn in the Province of West Prussia; some sources assign it to South Prussia. The 1793 map therefore only states 'incorporated into Prussia'.
